KoboldKare Mod Loader
=====================

Not affiliated with the developers of KoboldKare. You need your own copy of the game.

KoboldKare-ModLoader.zip bundles these, unchanged, under their own licenses (full texts in BepInEx\licenses):

- BepInEx 5 (BepInEx\core\BepInEx*.dll), LGPL-2.1
  https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx
- UnityDoorstop (winhttp.dll, doorstop_config.ini), LGPL-2.1
  https://github.com/NeighTools/UnityDoorstop
- HarmonyX (0Harmony*.dll, HarmonyXInterop.dll), MIT
  https://github.com/BepInEx/HarmonyX
- MonoMod (MonoMod.*.dll), MIT
  https://github.com/MonoMod/MonoMod
- Mono.Cecil (Mono.Cecil*.dll), MIT
  https://github.com/jbevain/cecil
- Mono class libraries (BepInEx\unstripped_corlib\mscorlib.dll, System.dll, System.Core.dll), MIT
  https://github.com/mono/mono
  KoboldKare ships stripped copies of these; BepInEx needs the full ones (doorstop's
  dll_search_path_override points at unstripped_corlib).

UnityEngine.AIModule.dll is Unity's and is not included. See README.md.

The Mod Manager, ModKit, NPC AI and UI Theme are made for this repository. The Night Sky theme is a
colours-only sample.

Scott's Tools uses the "Simplified RPG" 3D model and textures (2018) for its rocket launchers, resized and
embedded in KoboldKare.ScottsTools.dll.
